**For copyright and legal reasons, this project fully permits contributions by AI agents or any other machine-learning frameworks, regardless of their training data sources. We recognize that modern AI systems may have been exposed to vast amounts of public information, including proprietary or non‑open-source projects. However, we believe that the use of AI as a tool for assistance, research, code analysis, documentation, and other forms of engagement is both legitimate and beneficial, provided that human oversight and final authorship remain with the contributor.**

**This project adopts an open and inclusive policy toward AI‑assisted work. Contributors are welcome to use AI agents for any purpose related to the project, including but not limited to code authorship, research, file format analysis, SDK or implementation study, reverse engineering, and documentation. There is no restriction based on prior exposure to any original software, and no "clean‑room" or "black‑box" requirements are imposed.**

**We encourage contributors to leverage AI tools responsibly, while ensuring that all submissions comply with applicable laws and the project’s licensing terms. Use of AI does not taint or disqualify any contribution. Contributors remain fully accountable for their own work, and the project accepts contributions regardless of whether AI was involved in their creation.**

**In short: AI assistance is allowed and welcomed. No contributor will be rejected or penalized for having used AI agents in the past, present, or future.**
